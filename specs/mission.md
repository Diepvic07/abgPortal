# Mission

ABG Alumni Connect is the single digital home for ABG alumni community activity, combining events, proposals, reputation, news, member discovery, and AI-assisted matching in one trusted web platform.

## Objectives
- Help active ABG alumni discover relevant people, events, proposals, news, and contribution opportunities without relying on fragmented Zalo, Facebook, email, or ad hoc spreadsheets.
- Turn member intent into community action through proposal-to-event workflows, RSVP commitments, discussions, attendance tracking, and contribution scoring.
- Support a sustainable paid membership model that gives Free members a constrained preview and Pro members full access to search, matching, member visibility, references, and community participation benefits.
- Give administrators reliable tools for member approval, duplicate review, class management, content publishing, event operations, payment review, finance tracking, and moderation.

## Target Audience
- **Active ABG alumni**: Around 100 current active users who need a trusted place to participate in events, propose activities, find members, request introductions, and build reputation inside the community.
- **Full ABG alumni list**: Around 1000 known alumni profiles that form the broader directory and future activation pool.
- **ABG administrators and board members**: Operators who need structured workflows for approvals, events, proposals, payments, news, member data, and community health.
- **External visitors**: Public readers who access selected news, public event pages, guest RSVP flows, and public reference profiles where enabled.

## Success Definition
- Active member count grows and remains the primary product health metric.
- Members repeatedly use ABG Alumni Connect as the default place to discover events, proposals, people, news, and contribution opportunities.
- Admins can move community activity from idea to event to outcome with less manual coordination.
- Pro membership provides enough visible value to support the paid model while preserving trust, privacy, and community quality.
